**For Pedram to paste into App Store Connect (ASC), "App Review Information".** The block under "Paste this" is the Notes field (limit 4000 characters, checked by `docs/store/check-notes.mjs`). The demo credentials go in the separate Sign-in fields of ASC, **never in this repository**: the placeholders `<DEMO_EMAIL>` and `<DEMO_PASSWORD>` below stay placeholders.

## Before you paste (Pedram's checklist)

1. **The web deploy is done** (it carries `/delete-account` and the AASA file) and **build 3 is the build under review**.
2. **`account_deletion_enabled` is ON** in production (Admin → Feature flags, with a reason). It also gates the Security entry, the wizard account menu and the "under review" page's Delete link (`docs/runbooks/deploy-log.md:97,163`). With it off, the reviewer cannot find deletion and the app can be rejected under 5.1.1(v).
3. **The demo account exists and is verified by you**, built from `demo-account-runbook.md`, read back after building it.
4. **`push_notifications_enabled` read back** (it was armed on 2026-10-12): the notes say push is optional, so a reviewer who allows it should receive nothing odd.
5. **Flags that must be OFF** (not promised anywhere in the app or the listing): `contract_signing_enabled`, social OAuth, KYC, `account_erasure_execute_enabled`.
6. **Support and privacy pages are live** (`support-page.md`, `privacy-policy-app-section.md`), because the notes name them.
7. After the review: **rotate the demo password** and clean up reviewer-created accounts (runbook, last section).

## What is not claimed, on purpose

- No promise of payments, payouts, escrow, contract signing, social-account connect, or identity verification (all off or hidden; listing and notes match).
- No claim that the platform "moderates" message content. It does not scan or filter messages; the 1.2 argument is access control (vetting, connection, reporting, removal), not content filtering. If App Review asks for a block-user control, the answer is that a connection can be ended by us on a report; a user-side block is not built (a follow-up if review insists).
- No claim about age beyond the rating you enter. There is no minimum-age enforcement in the stack (`UpdateProfileRequest.php:67-71`, AH-088 D8); see `age-rating-answers.md` and `privacy-policy-app-section.md`.

### The AH-104e conflict (as you asked, recorded here and not in the policy)

The text says "**Security logs: retained, but personal details removed on erasure**", and lists "failed sign-ins" among what is logged. `account-erasure.md` §7 records the exception: **a failed login with the old address, after the erasure, writes the typed address and an ip into a new audit row that nothing scrubs** until AH-104e. So the sentence is true for every row that exists at erasure, and **false for any failed sign-in attempted afterwards with the erased address**. The gap is small (a stranger or the person typing the old address; the erased account's own email is replaced by `erased-{ulid}@erased.invalid`, so the old address no longer matches an account) but it is real. Decision for Pedram: accept it until AH-104e, or have the lawyer soften the sentence.
