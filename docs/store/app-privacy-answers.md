## Re-declare before these switch on

The label must be **updated before** any of these turn on (the declaration is a promise about the build that ships):

- **KYC (identity verification)**: government-ID data goes to a vendor. Re-declare (likely Other Data / Sensitive Info and Photos); also the vendor becomes a third party. Hidden build-wide today (`packages/api-client/src/wizard.ts:30`; `kyc_verification_enabled` off).
- **Tax profile and payout method**: financial details. Re-declare **Financial Info** (Payment Info). Hidden today (`WIZARD_HIDDEN_STEPS`; `creator_payout_method_enabled` off).
- **Social OAuth connect**: tokens and audience metrics from Instagram and TikTok. Re-declare (Other User Content / Usage Data) and the platforms as third parties.
- **Payments and escrow** (not built): Purchases and Financial Info, and likely guideline 3.1.1 questions.
- **Any analytics or crash reporter** (none today).
