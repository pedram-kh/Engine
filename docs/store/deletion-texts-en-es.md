## What the texts must agree with (the points to check while reading)

1. **"Permanently deleted on {date}" versus a manual erasure.** Three places say a date: section D `auth.ui.deletion.body` ("It will be permanently deleted on {date}"), section D `scheduled_banner` ("scheduled for deletion on {date}"), and section F `deletion_mail.body` ("It will be permanently deleted on :date, and this cannot be undone after that"). The engine does not erase on that date by itself: after the 14 days an admin clicks Erase (`docs/runbooks/account-erasure.md` §1), and the Monday routine (§1d) means up to about 7 further days. The privacy section promises "within one month of the grace ending" (`privacy-policy-app-section.md`). **Decision for you:** keep the wording (the date is when the grace ends and the account becomes erasable), or reword to "from {date}" / "on or after {date}" (an i18n change across 24 locales, and the mail in en and es plus the other API locales).
2. **"What stays" (section A `kept`)**: messages, applications, contracts and campaign records stay with the agencies, with the person's name removed. The policy section says the same; if you change one, change both.
3. **The 14 days** appear in A `intro` and `grace`, and in the mail. The privacy section and the review notes state 14 days too. One number, four places.
4. **Backups (7 days) and logs (up to 14 days)** are not mentioned in any in-app text. They are in the policy section (`account-erasure.md` §7). The in-app text says "Your name, email, ... and your sign-in details" are deleted, which is true of the live database; the policy qualifies it with the backup and log windows.
5. **"confirm with your password"** (E `inApp`) versus the dialog: the dialog asks for the word and an acknowledgement (B), **and** the current password, plus the authenticator or recovery code when two-factor is on (`apps/main/src/modules/creators/components/DeleteAccountDialog.vue:10,47-74`). So E is accurate but incomplete (it omits the typed word and the code); fine for a short public page, noted for completeness.
6. **The support address** in C `contact` and E `support` is `{email}`, filled from `SUPPORT_EMAIL` (`apps/main/src/core/config/support.ts:10`, default `support@catalyst-growth.com`). The app does not hard-code the address in any text.
7. **Other API locales.** The mail exists in all 24 API locales (`apps/api/lang/*/auth.php`); this sheet shows en and es only. The flaky ten (`bg el et fi ga hu lt lv mt ro`) are machine translations: not part of this read-through.
