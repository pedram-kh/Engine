# Age rating answers (AH-105)

**For Pedram to enter in App Store Connect (ASC) → App Information → Age Rating.** The answers are drafted **from the code**. **ASC computes the tier from the answers; I do not.** After you enter them, **read the tier ASC shows and report it** (Q4, ruled): the "17+" in D9 is the intent (adults only), not a tier I can promise ASC will print. Apple's questionnaire and tier names have been revised more than once; **the question wording and the tier set below are from memory**: read each screen and adapt.

## Intent (D9, ruled 2026-10-29)

Catalyst is for **adults**. The age rating should say so, and **one sentence in the privacy policy says the app is intended for people aged 18 or over** (`privacy-policy-app-section.md`). **There is no enforcement behind either**: the date of birth is collected but there is **no minimum-age rule anywhere in the stack** (`apps/api/app/Modules/Creators/Http/Requests/UpdateProfileRequest.php:66-71`; the record is `docs/reviews/creator-dob-age-review.md:77`, D8: "No minimum-age rule anywhere. Offered and declined."). Neither the rating nor the sentence is a control. They are a statement of intent, and the record of that is **AH-088 D8** (the 18+ sentence stays with the no-enforcement record, Q5).

## What to do with the result

1. Enter the answers. Note **the exact tier ASC displays** and send it back; I will record it in the AH-105 entry.
2. If ASC shows a tier **lower than adults-only** and you want the adult statement in the rating itself, ASC has an age-rating override (from memory; check the screen): set it to the highest tier on offer and note it as the D9 intent. It remains a statement, not a control.
3. Do **not** change the questionnaire to push the tier up (answering "frequent" content that does not exist): reviewers compare answers with the app.
