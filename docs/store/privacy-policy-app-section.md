## Catalyst Creators app

This section applies when you use the Catalyst Creators app for iPhone. The rest of this policy continues to apply.

### Who is responsible

[Legal company name], [registered address], is the data controller. Contact: support@catalyst-growth.com.

### What we collect

- **Account:** name, email address, password (stored in hashed form), two-factor authentication settings, email verification status.
- **Profile:** profile photo, date of birth, phone or WhatsApp number, postal address, social-media account names and follower counts, portfolio items and availability. These are provided by you or by the agency you work with.
- **Work:** your agency relationships, campaign applications, assignments, contracts and the messages you exchange with agencies and brands.
- **Device and notifications:** a push-notification token for your device, your notification preferences, the app version, operating system and device language.
- **Technical and security:** IP address and app or browser identifier in security logs (sign-ins, failed sign-ins and changes to your account).

We do not collect precise location, contacts, health data or advertising identifiers. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell personal data.

### Why we use it and the legal basis

- To provide the service: your account, your work with agencies, messaging and notifications (performance of a contract).
- To keep the service secure: sign-in protection, fraud prevention and security logs (legitimate interest).
- To meet legal duties, for example tax and record-keeping obligations where they apply (legal obligation).
- Push notifications are sent only with your permission. You can withdraw it at any time in the app's notification settings or in iOS Settings (consent).

### Who processes it for us

- Amazon Web Services: hosting and file storage, EU region.
- Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and Apple Push Notification service: delivery of push notifications. They receive your device token and the notification text.
- Postmark: transactional email.

Each provider acts under a data-processing agreement. Where a provider processes data outside the EU or UK, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

### How long we keep it

- Account and profile data: while your account is active.
- Encrypted backups: up to 7 days after deletion.
- Server logs: up to 14 days.
- Security logs: retained, but personal details in them are removed when your account is erased.
- Records required by law (for example tax records): for the legally required period.

### Deleting your account

You can request deletion in the app (Settings → Delete account) or at https://platform.catalyst-growth.com/delete-account. Your account is locked immediately and hidden from new activity. You have 14 days to change your mind by signing in again. After that period your personal data is permanently erased within one month. Agencies you worked with keep their campaign and contract records, with your name and details replaced by "[erased]". Some records may be kept where the law requires it.

### Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict processing, and withdraw consent, through the app's settings or by emailing support@catalyst-growth.com. You can also complain to the Spanish data-protection authority (AEPD, www.aepd.es) or to the authority in your country.

### Age

The Catalyst Creators app is intended for people aged 18 or over.
