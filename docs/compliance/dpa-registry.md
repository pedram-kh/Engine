# DPA registry

Created 2026-11-15 (AH-121 C4). `docs/05-SECURITY-COMPLIANCE.md` §2.6 says every processor's data-processing agreement (DPA) is "tracked in `docs/compliance/dpa-registry.md`"; the file did not exist. **Engineering record, not legal advice.** A row says what someone reported; it is not a legal review and does not prove the agreement says what the privacy texts say.

**Rule (ruling Q10, AH-121):** the DPA is accepted **before the first real DSN, key or credential is set anywhere** for that processor.

**Only the Sentry row holds a recorded fact.** Every other row is **not recorded**: the repo contains no record of the agreement, its date or who accepted it. That includes processors the privacy texts already say act "under a data-processing agreement" (`docs/store/privacy-policy-app-section.md`). Filling those rows is an open item for Pedram; nothing here says they are missing, only that nothing was read.

## How to use it

1. Before a processor's first real credential goes anywhere, add or fill its row: who accepted, when, where the signed or accepted copy is kept (do not paste the agreement or any secret here).
2. Whoever changes what a processor receives updates the "What it receives" cell in the same change, and the privacy texts (`docs/store/`).
3. A row is written from a report or a read, and says which. Never "accepted" without a source.
