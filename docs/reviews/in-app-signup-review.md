## 3. Behaviour to read before you clear it

- **What the limiter costs.** Five attempts per normalised email per hour, including failed ones: a person who mistypes the password five times is locked out of **signing up with that address** for up to an hour
  (not out of an existing account). A second person can burn an address's hour by posting five requests for it. No account is created and nothing is mailed on a 429. There is still **no bot control beyond the
  two limiters** (`auth-ip` 10 per minute per IP, this one); a bot with fresh addresses is limited by IP only. Tech-debt row.
- **The pending page's button is app-only and has two states.** It is the only way back from the pending screen inside the app, because the back link is hidden there. A creator who signed up on the phone and
  verified in Safari taps it and lands on sign-in with the email filled in; a creator who is still signed in (an unverified sign-in) is checked against the server and sent to the wizard without a password.
- **No auto-advance.** The page does not notice verification by itself when the app comes to the foreground. Tech-debt row (a `watch` on the resume refresh would do it).
- **Security from an unverified account.** The link exists on the signed-in pending page only, and only while `account_deletion_enabled` is on. Whether the **deletion request itself works for an unverified
  account** is a server question I did not change; W10 on the walk is the proof.
- **The consent line is a notice.** Nothing records that it was shown or read. There is no `terms_accepted_at`.
- **Invited creators stay web-only** (Q5). The `?token=` path in `SignUpPage` still works on the web and the invitation mail links open in a browser; nothing in the app can reach it.
- **Locale on the native wizard is still open** (AH-101 Q10): the sign-up sheet has the language pill (the sheet layout draws it); the wizard still has none.
